## How to Approach This Task

1. Identify the core strategic question embedded in the task
2. List the 2–3 most significant uncertainties or unknowns
3. Evaluate the 2–3 most plausible paths forward against: mission alignment, resource feasibility, risk level
4. Produce a clear recommendation with supporting rationale
5. Identify any decisions that require human confirmation before proceeding

---

## Output Format

Lead with a one-sentence recommendation.
Follow with structured reasoning (use headers for each key point).
End with a list of: open questions, human-confirmation needs, and recommended next agent tasks.

Do not pad — every sentence must carry meaning.
Do not hedge every claim — assign confidence levels explicitly where uncertainty matters.
